# 📊 Customer & Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive Customer & Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Tableau and Excel datasets. This project provides insights into sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and business growth through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
* Compare Current Year (CY) and Previous Year (PY) performance.
* Identify top customers and products.
* Support data-driven business decision making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Tableau
* Microsoft Excel
* CSV Files
* JSON Data
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization
* KPI Analysis

## 📂 Dataset Files

* Customers.csv
* Customers_Details.csv
* Orders.csv
* Products.csv
* Product_prices.json
* LOD.csv

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Customer Dashboard

* Total Customers KPI
* Customer Growth Analysis
* Monthly Customer Trends
* Customer Segmentation
* Top Customers Analysis

### Sales Dashboard

* Total Sales KPI
* Total Profit KPI
* Total Orders KPI
* Sales Growth %
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Product Performance Analysis
* Regional Sales Analysis
* Top Products by Revenue

## 📊 Key Insights

* Track customer acquisition and retention trends.
* Analyze sales and profit performance over time.
* Identify high-value customers and products.
* Compare business performance between CY and PY.
* Discover growth opportunities through interactive analytics.
